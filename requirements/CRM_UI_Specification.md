
# CRM UI Specification

## UI-01 Login
Fields: Username, Password, SSO, MFA Code, Remember Me.
Actions: Login, Forgot Password.

## UI-02 Home Dashboard
Widgets: KPI Cards, Pipeline, Revenue, Forecast, My Tasks, My Calendar, AI Recommendations, Recent Activities.
Actions: Filter by Date, Sales, Team, Export.

## UI-03 Customer List
Grid: Customer Code, Company, Industry, Status, Owner, Last Activity.
Actions: Search, Filter, Sort, Add, Edit, Delete, Import Excel, Export Excel/PDF/Word, Merge Duplicate.

## UI-04 Customer Detail
Tabs: Profile, Contacts, Opportunities, Activities, Documents, Quotations, Timeline, AI Summary.
Actions: Edit, Upload, Add Contact, Create Opportunity.

## UI-05 Lead
Grid: Lead, Source, Score, Owner, Status.
Actions: AI Import, Manual Add, Convert, Assign, Bulk Update.

## UI-06 Opportunity
Pipeline View, Kanban View, List View.
Fields: Stage, Revenue, Probability, Close Date, Products.
Actions: Move Stage, Forecast, AI Next Best Action.

## UI-07 Activity & Calendar
Views: Day, Week, Month.
Actions: Meeting, Call, Visit, Reminder, AI Meeting Summary.

## UI-08 Quotation
Header, Customer, Product Grid, Discount, Tax, Approval Status.
Actions: Generate PDF, Email, AI Draft Proposal.

## UI-09 Document Center
Upload Area, OCR Result, Preview, Version History.
Actions: OCR, AI Extract, Download.

## UI-10 Reports
Filters, Charts, Tables.
Actions: Export Excel, PDF, Word, Schedule Report.

## UI-11 AI Workspace
Panels: AI Chat, Lead Hunter, Tender Search, Document AI, Meeting AI.
Actions: Run Agent, Save Result, Create CRM Record.

## UI-12 Mobile
Bottom Navigation: Home, Customer, Lead, Opportunity, Task.
Support: Camera, Voice, GPS, Offline Draft, Push Notification.

Common UI:
Global Search, Notification, Profile, Breadcrumb, Responsive Layout, Light/Dark Mode, Role-based Menu.
